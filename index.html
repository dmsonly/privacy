DMs Only Android Privacy Policy

Updated October 24, 2026

Scope and contact

This policy covers DMs Only for Android, package com.dmsonly.app. DMs Only opens Instagram's website in a focused WebView with the Feed, Reels browsing and Explore hidden. DMs Only is independent of Instagram and Meta.

For questions or requests about data held by DMs Only, contact reshu70173@gmail.com.

Instagram sign-in and content

You use your existing Instagram account. Sign-in credentials are submitted to Instagram on its own website inside the app. DMs Only does not send your Instagram password, message contents or Instagram session cookies to any DMs Only server.

The embedded Instagram website communicates directly with Meta to provide your account, messages, Stories, profile and activity. Information you enter or send there, including your account identifiers, messages and selected media, is processed by Meta. The website can also process IP addresses, browser and device information, cookies, approximate location inferred from IP addresses, interactions and diagnostics for service operation, security, personalization, analytics and advertising under Meta's policy.

Hiding parts of the Instagram interface does not stop Meta from processing information when you use Instagram. Instagram may show its own ads in the web content. Its handling of information is described in Meta's Privacy Policy.

Unread message notifications

When unread notifications are turned on, DMs Only checks about every 15 minutes how many of your Instagram conversations are unread. It does this with a single request from your device directly to Instagram, using the Instagram session already stored in the app. The result is used only to show a notification on your device, and the latest count is stored locally. Nothing is sent to any other server. You can turn this off in DMs Only's Settings or block DMs Only's notifications in Android Settings.

Information stored on your device

Android WebView stores Instagram cookies and website data to keep your session available. DMs Only stores onboarding progress, screen-time answers, preferences and the latest unread count locally. The app does not upload these onboarding answers.

DMs Only does not include a billing SDK, an advertising SDK or any usage-analytics integration. It does not request access to your address book or device location.

DMs Only asks for camera or microphone access only when Instagram's website requests it for a feature you choose to use, such as a voice message or video call. This access is offered only to Instagram's own pages, and the audio or video goes directly to Instagram. You can deny these permissions or revoke them in Android Settings at any time. Files you choose through a system file picker are handled by the feature you choose to use on Instagram.

Optional Instagram blocking

If you separately agree to Instagram blocking and enable DMs Only's Accessibility service, DMs Only uses Android's Accessibility API to detect which app is open and show a blocking screen over the Instagram app. It does not retrieve screen contents, messages, passwords or keystrokes through this service. App detection is processed on the device and is not stored or shared.

Your blocking preference, permission consent, daily unlock count and temporary unlock expiry are stored locally. You can skip this feature, turn off blocking in DMs Only's Settings, or revoke Accessibility access in Android Settings at any time. This feature does not block access to Android Settings or prevent uninstalling DMs Only.

Network connections and support

DMs Only does not operate its own servers. The app connects only to Instagram's website and the Meta services it loads, including the unread check described above. It does not download configuration files from DMs Only or any other provider.

If you email support, we receive the address and information you choose to send. We use this to respond to your request and retain it as needed to handle the request, resolve issues or meet legal obligations. Please do not send passwords or private message contents.

Security and sharing

DMs Only uses HTTPS for its connections to Instagram. Encryption in transit does not mean all Instagram messages are end-to-end encrypted. DMs Only does not sell your personal information. Instagram processes the information you provide to its service and uses its own service providers under Meta's policy.

Your choices and deletion

To remove DMs Only's local preferences and WebView data, use Android Settings → Apps → DMs Only → Storage → Clear storage (the names may vary by phone). This signs you out locally and resets DMs Only. It does not delete your Instagram account or messages held by Meta.

To manage Instagram sessions, content or account deletion, use Instagram's settings and Accounts Center. Review Instagram's account deletion instructions. DMs Only cannot delete an Instagram account on your behalf.

DMs Only does not keep records about you on any server. To request deletion of support messages, email reshu70173@gmail.com from the relevant email address; do not send your password. Meta controls retention of information held by Instagram.

Age and policy changes

DMs Only is not intended for children under 13. You must meet Instagram's minimum age requirement in your country. We may update this policy when the Android app's data handling changes; the effective date above identifies the latest revision.
